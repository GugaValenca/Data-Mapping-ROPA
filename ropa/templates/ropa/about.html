About this project

What this is

Data-Mapping-ROPA is a working Record of Processing Activities (ROPA) tool for a fictional mid-size e-commerce company, "NimbusCart." Instead of the industry-standard static spreadsheet or Word document, processing activities are stored as structured, related database records — data categories, legal bases, recipients, and retention periods are all reusable building blocks — and the ROPA report, along with its PDF/CSV exports, is generated automatically from that data.

Why I built it

I'm a Brazilian-trained lawyer with legal team leadership experience, transitioning into Data Governance & Privacy Analyst work in the US. The ROPA is too often treated as paperwork: a Word document assembled once for an audit and rarely touched again. I wanted to build the alternative: the actual system a privacy team runs on, where adding an activity is a form, not a new spreadsheet row buried in someone's inbox, and where a documentation gap (sensitive data with no defined legal basis or retention period) surfaces on its own instead of waiting to be found during an audit.

It's the operational counterpart to a separate project in this portfolio that compares LGPD, GDPR, and CCPA side by side: that one demonstrates the legal-analysis skill, this one demonstrates the "build the tool the team actually runs on" skill.

How it works

  • Privacy/compliance staff maintain activities through Django admin, the same way a real team would update records over time.
  • The dashboard renders the current ROPA as a filterable, searchable table — by data category, legal basis, or cross-border transfer.
  • Any activity combining a sensitive data category with an undefined legal basis or retention period is flagged automatically.
  • The full (or filtered) ROPA can be exported as a ready-to-hand-over PDF or CSV at any time.

Tech stack

Django · SQLite (dev) · Django templates + vanilla CSS/JS · ReportLab (PDF) · Python csv module (CSV)

Scope & disclaimer

All company, department, and vendor names are fictional. Legal basis labels are generic categories (Consent, Contract Necessity, Legitimate Interest, Legal Obligation), not legal advice for any specific situation. Retention periods shown are illustrative placeholders pending verification against actual law and are marked as such in the source code — they are not asserted as legally required durations.
